Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09 November 2026

Who we are

AXON Systems builds AI systems for hotels and is based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: gulcanyesilbudak@icloud.com.

What we collect

When you send the demo request form we receive the details you enter: name, business email, hotel name, number of rooms, main challenge, and optionally your WhatsApp number, package of interest and message. We use them only to reply to your request and to prepare a proposal.

The interactive demo assistant on the homepage runs in your browser and does not store or send what you type. If live AI mode is switched on, your demo messages are sent to our AI provider only to generate the reply and are not stored by us.

Cookies and analytics

We store your language choice in your browser. If we enable Google Analytics, it only loads after you accept it in the banner, and we do not send your name, email or phone number to it.

Service providers

The website is hosted on Vercel. Form messages are delivered to our inbox through an email delivery provider. These providers process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep demo requests as long as needed to follow up and for our business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your rights

Under KVKK and, where it applies, the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Write to gulcanyesilbudak@icloud.com and we will reply within 30 days.
