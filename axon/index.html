The problem

How Many Guest Inquiries Are You Missing?

Guests write when it suits them, not when your team is free. When a reply comes late, the booking often goes to another hotel or through a commission-based channel.

01

Missed inquiries after hours

A guest writing at night usually contacts several hotels at once. The property that gives the first clear answer is the one most likely to win the booking.

02

Slow responses

Potential guests compare options while they wait. Every hour without a reply is time to book somewhere else, often through an online travel agency.

03

Repetitive questions

Check-in times, parking, breakfast, transfers. Your reception team spends the shift answering the same messages instead of looking after the guests in front of them.

04

Language barriers

A message in a language nobody on shift speaks waits for the right colleague, or gets a short reply. The guest's confidence drops before the stay has even started.
